Kpop

K-pop is Korean pop that combies music with fashion and dancing

It originated from South Korea and emerged in the early 1990s, now having 5 different generation of K-pop. It is typically included by contemparary R&B, Hip-pop, EDM, rock, and dance music.

In the 2010s social media boosted the itnernational popularity of K-pop

By 2019, South Korea ranked sixth among music markets, led by artists, becoming the fastest growing music market

The main 4 industry companies include: SM, JYP, YG, and Hybe. A lot of K-pop companies are critized for their treatment of idols.

K-pop Idols:

To become a K-pop idol, you have to do a lot of vocal and dance training along with making sure that you are ready to preform and have a good public image

Some k-pop idols dont speak Korean so they must learn this, and they usually have to know English atleast a little bit, they also tend to be young teenagers when they start training

During their training period, they will have 8-12 hour training days as well as monthly evaluations to assess your skills.

During the training period, you are known as a trainee and tend to live with your other trainees. Some groups will train everyone for the group while other groups do a elimation style to get to theiur final group

This is not always the case however in rare examples. Stray Kids for example did an emlimation style but instead of the industry picking the members, the leader Bang Chan handpicked the members

Another common thing among K-pop idols is either leaving their group or going on hiatus. Hiatus usually happens due to injury or mental health, which many tend to have mental health issue due to industry standards

You will find that more often then not, K-pop groups will have atleast one member that has left the group or is on Hiatus

A small note is that all K-pop groups have a Maknae, which is the youngest member
