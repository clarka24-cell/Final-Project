K-pop: LE SSERAFIM

About LE SSERAFIM:

LE SSERAFIM is a fourth generation K-pop girl group with 5 members that debut in 2022 under the HYBE agency.

They make dance-pop style music and are known to be a powerhouse among the fourth generation K-pop groups.

LE SSERAFIM Members:

  • Sakura - Vocalist, dancer. She originally debuted at 13 years old, making LE SSERAFIM her 3rd group debut.
  • Kim Chaewon - Leader, lead dancer, main vocalist. She is the youngest daughter of well-known South Korean theater actress Lee Ran-hee as well as having debuted in one group before LE SSERAFIM.
  • Huh Yunjin - vocalist. She is heavily trained in opera singing and musical theater.
  • Kazuha - Lead dancer, vocalist, rapper. SHe was professionaly trained in Ballet , dancing for 15 years.
  • Hong Eunchae - vocalist, Maknae. She served as a co-host for KBS's Music Bank for a year and a half after LE SSERAFIMs debut.
