About ENHYPEN:

ENHYPEN is a fourth generation K-pop boy band with 6 current members that debut in 2020.

They make hip-pop style music and are known for their dark fantasy concepts.

ENHYPEN Members:

  • Ni-ki
  • Sunghoon
  • Sunoo
  • Jungwon
  • Jay
  • Jake
  • Former member: Evan
