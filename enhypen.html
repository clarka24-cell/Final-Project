K-pop: ENHYPEN

About ENHYPEN:

ENHYPEN is a fourth generation K-pop boy band with 6 current members that debut in 2020.

They make hip-pop style music and are known for their dark fantasy concepts.

ENHYPEN Members and some fun facts:

  • Ni-ki - Main dancer, vocalist, Maknae. He was a backup dancer for SHINee in 2016-2017.
  • Sunghoon - Vocalist, dancer, visual. He was a competitive figure skater before his debut, winning silver and gold medals at the Asian Open Figure Skating.
  • Sunoo - vocalist, dancer, rapper. He underwent thoracic surgery for a pneumothorax (collapsed lung) in 2019 and had to heavily train his lung capacity
  • Jungwon - Leader, vocalist, dancer. He practiced and competed in Taekwondo for many years.
  • Jay - vocalist, dancer, rapper. He was born in the United States, and moved to South Korean at 9 years old.
  • Jake - vocalist, dancer, rapper. He excelled in school and played the violin for 4 years.
  • Former member: Evan - Left in May 2026 to persue solo career
