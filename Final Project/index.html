Discover K-pop

K-pop is Korean pop that combies music with fashion and dancing

It originated from South Korea and emerged in the early 1990s, now having 5 different generation of K-pop. It is typically included by contemparary R&B, Hip-pop, EDM, rock, and dance music.

The main 4 industry companies include: SM, JYP, YG, and Hybe.
