K-pop: Stray Kids

About Stray Kids:

Stray Kids is a fourth generation K-pop boy band with 8 current members that debut in 2018. They are often known for their connection with fans through their live streams and STAYweeK during the week they debuted each year.

They make "mala" style music, which is an intense, high-energy, and addictive style of K-pop that was pioneered by Stray Kids.

Stray Kids Members and some fun facts:

  • Bang Chan - Leader, producer, dancer, vocalist, rapper. Before his debut, he was a competitive swimmer in Australia.
  • Felix - dancer, rapper, visual. He is known for his deep voice, and was eliminated from his survival show, but was brought back due to popular request.
  • Hyunjin - Main dancer, rapper, visual. Lived in Las Vegas as a kid and was scouted by JYP when he was out shopping with his mother.
  • Han - main rapper, lead vocalist, producer. He spent his childhood in Malaysia, making him fluent in English.
  • Lee Know - Main dancer, vocalist, visual. He was a BTS backup dancer for some tours and music videos before his debut.
  • Seungmin - Main vocalist. In 2013, a helicopter crashed into his apartment building, but he still went to schoo.
  • I.N - Vocalist, Maknae. He developed his love of singing through singing at church events.
  • Changbin - Main rapper, producer. He is a full member of the Korea Music Copyright Association, with over 200 registered song credits
  • Former Member: Kim Woojin - Left in October 2019
