K-pop: BLACKPINK

About BLACKPINK:

BLACKPINK is a third generation K-pop girl group with 4 members that debut in 2016.

They make hip-pop and EDM style music and are known for their "Girl Crush" concept of femininity and power.

The members of BLACKPINK also have very strong individual careers outside of their group.

BLACKPINK Members and some fun facts:

  • Lisa - Main dancer, Maknae. She is not Korean, she was born is Thailand. She has songs in English, Thai, and Korean.
  • Jennie - Main rapper, lead vocalist. She is a fashion icon, having models Chanel, Adidas, Calvin Klein, and other famous brands.
  • Rose - Main vocalist, lead dancer. Her ethnicity is Korean, however she was born is New Zealand.
  • Jisoo - Lead vocalist, Visual. She is known as the joker of the group, often making dad jokes.
